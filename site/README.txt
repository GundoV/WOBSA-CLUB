WHAT'S INCLUDED
- index.html            One-page site: Home, About, Departments, Gallery,
                         Community Impact, Highlights (video), Sponsors,
                         Leadership, Membership + sign-up form, Contact,
                         Footer.
- assets/images/        Your race-day photos, already wired into the page
                         (hero, about, gallery, community, sponsors,
                         leadership) plus a logo cropped from your
                         sponsor certificate artwork.
- assets/videos/        Your race highlight clip, embedded in the
                         Highlights section with playback controls.

HOW TO VIEW IT
Double-click index.html to open it in any browser. To put it online,
upload the whole folder (index.html + assets) to your web host exactly
as it is — file paths are relative, so nothing needs to change.

THE MEMBERSHIP FORM NOW ACTUALLY SENDS EMAIL — ONE STEP TO ACTIVATE
The sign-up form posts to a free service called FormSubmit
(https://formsubmit.co), addressed to info@wobsa.co.za. No account,
password or backend needed. To switch it on:
  1. Publish the site (or just open index.html locally) and submit the
     form once yourself with test details.
  2. FormSubmit will send an activation email to info@wobsa.co.za
     titled "Confirm form submission on FormSubmit.co" — someone with
     access to that inbox needs to click the confirmation link inside.
  3. Every submission after that lands directly in info@wobsa.co.za as
     a neatly formatted table (name, DOB, email, phone, department,
     tier, emergency contact, notes).
Until step 2 is done, the first (test) submission will not arrive by
email — only the activation email will. All later submissions work
automatically. If you'd rather send sign-ups to a different address,
change info@wobsa.co.za in the form's "action" attribute in index.html.

STILL NOT POSSIBLE WITH A PLAIN HTML FILE
Taking membership payments online still needs a South African payment
gateway (PayFast, Yoco, PayGate) wired in by a developer or your
hosting provider — FormSubmit only handles the sign-up notification,
not payment collection. Happy to help with that when you're ready.
