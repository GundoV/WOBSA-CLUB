Who we are

Athletics is one department. Community is the whole point.

Formally constituted on 26 January 2021 and based at Kwamashu Centre, opposite Phangami Shopping Mall in Maniini, Thohoyandou, WOBSA exists to form, administer and finance sports departments for our members and the wider community — while keeping proper playing fields, facilities and coaching in place for everyone who wants to take part.

We don't discriminate on the grounds of sex, age, disability, ethnicity, nationality, sexual orientation, religion or other beliefs. Every rand of surplus income gets reinvested in the academy — there are no profits paid out to individuals.

  • Six sporting departments, each with its own elected management team
  • Annual and honorary membership open to the community
  • Organisers of the NHC-WOBSA Development Road Race, run under WA, ASA and LIMA rules
  • A dedicated Disability & Elderly Officer and Youth & Children's Officer on our committee
Our sporting departments

Six codes, one academy

Every department runs as its own club within WOBSA, with an elected committee overseeing day-to-day training and events.

Athletics

Track, field & road running

From club time trials to provincial road races — our largest department, and the one behind the NHC-WOBSA Development Road Race.

Football

Football

Local league matches and structured tactical training for players building toward competitive football.

Netball

Netball

Team play, agility and match fitness for our netball squads across age groups.

Cricket

Cricket

Batting, bowling and match strategy coaching for players developing their game.

Rugby

Rugby

Strength, resilience and discipline on the field, built through structured team training.

Rowing

Indoor & water rowing

Our newest department, expanding the academy into indoor and water-based endurance fitness.
